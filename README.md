# AJG-ERPResearch
Independent Research towards the Beacon Capstone Project.
This project serves as the technology stack research project for the Beacon capstone project tentatively named "NetServe Pro".  
I will aim to compare the next.js and spring boot implementation against my original suggestion of Blazor and ASP.NET, aiming to understand the advantages and disadvantages of the two projects.
  Current selection of next.js and spring boot are choices made due to team familiarity over any real functional gain between the two.
  My initial suggestion in utilizing Blazor and ASP.NET exist for two fundamental reasons, the first being a simple transition between all device platforms through .NET MAUI and
  the second reason being it's direct inter-connectivity with Microsoft Business Platforms that exist as a primary business platform for many business, opening availability of services like:
    Sharepoint: CRM document libraries for photo/document storage.
    Dataverse: Cloud based data storage with advanced data modeling.
    Entra:   Tennent based login authorization secured by Microsofts authorization/authentication services (Improved/Outsourced Security).

If time permits, I would also like to make a comparison of the functionality behind utilizing SQL versus Dataverse as the primary data storage mechanism.
  SQL is fast and responsive, but the advanced modeling and analytics properties of Dataverse is likely to serve a company with this data set far more than simple functional data storage.


  The idea will be to build a simple service tickets scheduling slice with varied components to determine the strengths and weaknesses of the two architectures. 

  Time permitting, I may venture to expand into additional features to make additional conclusions about the two architectures.
